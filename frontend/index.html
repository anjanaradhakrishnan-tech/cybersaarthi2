Cyber Safety Education

🔑 OTP Scams

Scammers call claiming to be bank employees or delivery agents asking for your OTP to verify details.

Rule: NEVER share your OTP with anyone over a call or text message.

📲 UPI Refund Fraud

Scammers claim they accidentally transferred money to your UPI account and ask you to scan a QR code or enter your PIN to refund it.

Rule: Entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS deducts money from your account. You NEVER enter a PIN to receive money.

🏛 Fake KYC & Account Block

Urgent SMS stating your bank account, SIM card, or PAN will be deactivated unless you click a link immediately.

Rule: Banks never update KYC via random SMS links. Visit your official bank branch.

🎭 AI Deepfakes & Impersonation

AI clone videos or voice calls pretending to be your relative in trouble demanding emergency money transfers.

Rule: Always call back your relative on their saved phone number directly to verify.
