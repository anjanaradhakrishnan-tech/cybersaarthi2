# CyberSaarthi User Guide

Welcome to **CyberSaarthi – Cybersecurity Without Digital Exclusion**.

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. How to Check a Suspicious Message or Link
1. Open the CyberSaarthi homepage.
2. Click **Check a Message** or **Check a Link**.
3. Paste the text or URL you received.
4. Click **Analyze Risk**.
5. Read the Risk Score (`LOW RISK`, `SUSPICIOUS`, or `HIGH RISK`), detected threat list, and recommended action.

### 2. How to Enable Elder Mode
1. Click the **👴 Elder Mode** toggle button in the header bar.
2. The platform will automatically increase font size (22px+), enhance contrast, enlarge buttons, and simplify safety instructions.
3. Click the speaker button for voice reading of warnings.

### 3. What to do in an Emergency ("I THINK I AM BEING SCAMMED")
1. Click the red **🚨 I THINK I AM BEING SCAMMED** button.
2. Follow the 5 immediate steps:
   - Stop communicating with the scammer.
   - Do NOT share OTP, PIN, or passwords.
   - Contact your bank / National Helpline **1930** immediately.
   - Take screenshots of messages and numbers.
   - Click **CALL CYBERSAARTHI HELPLINE** to dispatch a helpline call.
